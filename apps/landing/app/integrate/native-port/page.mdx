# Native port

Port the flow into your own components when your docs host allows neither iframes nor third-party scripts, so neither the [hosted iframe](/integrate/iframe) nor the [self-hosted widget](/integrate/self-hosted) is an option. You rebuild what the flow computes with `@solana/kit`, in your own framework and styles. Nothing loads from plgrnd.io.

The trade-off: the port is your code, not a dependency. You write it, test it and maintain it, and fixes to PLGRND never reach it.

## How to port a flow

1. Open the flow in PLGRND and list its nodes: the inputs (TEXT, KEYPAIR) and what they feed (PDA).
2. Map every node to a `@solana/kit` call: a PDA node is `getProgramDerivedAddress`, a KEYPAIR node is `generateKeyPair` plus `getAddressFromPublicKey`. Encode text seeds as UTF-8 bytes and public keys with `getAddressEncoder`.
3. Validate inputs the way the flow does: `isAddress` for program ids and public keys, at most 32 bytes per seed.
4. Compare the port's outputs with the flow's for the same inputs, for example `seeds = []` gives bump 253.
5. Render the port on the client only, and put the credit line directly below it.
